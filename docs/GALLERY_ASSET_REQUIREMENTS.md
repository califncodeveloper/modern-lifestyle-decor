## 4. Photography & Asset Specifications for Business Team

When capturing on-site installation photographs for future upload:

1. **Lighting**: Capture during daytime with natural ambient illumination and interior fixtures turned on. Avoid harsh direct flash on fabrics and reflective aluminium surfaces.
2. **Composition**:
   - Provide a mix of full-room architectural context (showing how the blinds/curtains frame the space) and medium detail shots (highlighting finish, track alignment, and mesh tension).
3. **Resolution**: Minimum `1920 × 1280 px` (recommended `2400 × 1600 px` or higher) in sRGB color space.
4. **File Format**: High-quality JPEG, WebP, or PNG without watermarks (branding and watermarks are applied systematically).
5. **Directory Placement**: Once provided, files must be placed directly into `public/images/projects/` following the filename conventions listed above.
